    🔑 Numbers worth memorizing

    • 1 gpg = 17.1 ppm as CaCO₃  ·  1 lb = 7,000 grains
    • Mass balance: lb/day = ppm × gal/day × 8.34 ÷ 1,000,000
    • Heat: BTU/hr = gpm × ΔT°F × 500 (water)
    • 1 BHP = 34.5 lb steam/hr ≈ 0.069 gpm feedwater
    • Tower evaporation ≈ 1% of recirc per 10 °F of range
    • Tower ton = 15,000 BTU/hr · 3 gpm recirc per ton
    • Blowdown (% of steam) = 100 ÷ (COC − 1)
    • RO: ~1 psi osmotic pressure per 100 ppm TDS
